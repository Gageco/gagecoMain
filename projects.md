## Analog Dither

<span class="image left"><img src="{{ "./images/AnalogDither.png" | absolute_url }}" alt="" /></span>

Analog Dithers first signed with Andor Records in 2018 and now has over 750k streams on Spotify alone! Their first release 'Pole Position' is an absolute banger and is their most popular song released to date.

Those who like eclectic music, and instrumentals with just a little bit of strange overtones will like the music of Analog Dither. They can be found on all big streaming platforms and many smaller ones as well as Bandcamp at [analogdither.bandcamp.com](https://analogdither.bandcamp.com)

Contact [Analog Dither](mailto:analog@andorrecords.com)
